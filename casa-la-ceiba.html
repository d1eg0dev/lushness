The Villa

Between the jungle and the sea.

A private oceanfront estate built for gathering — and for the celebrations that deserve a setting of their own.

Part of the exclusive international community of Costa Careyes, the house sits in absolute privacy, its architecture in quiet harmony with the landscape. Infinity views, a full staff and grounds that open straight onto the Pacific.

It is the home of our destination weddings, hosting up to 18 guests on-site and celebrations well beyond — from the ceremony itself to guest stays and full production, exactly as far as you want to take it.

The Setting

Costa Careyes, since 1968.

Careyes is a private community on Mexico's Pacific coast, founded in 1968 — architecture, art and the ocean conceived as one. Reached by jungle road and private gates, roughly 90 minutes from Manzanillo International Airport, it keeps the outside world at a comfortable distance.
