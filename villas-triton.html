Costa Careyes, since 1968.

Careyes is a private community on Mexico's Pacific coast, founded in 1968 — architecture, art and the ocean conceived as one. Reached by jungle road and private gates, roughly 90 minutes from Manzanillo International Airport, it keeps the outside world at a comfortable distance.
